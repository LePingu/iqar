## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one polished new logo for IQAR, a financial analytics and trading platform. Combine an abstract Icarus wing with a financial performance curve in a single coherent, distinctive symbol. Three elegant tapered feather strokes sweep upward; one continuous curved line has subtle market-like rises and pullbacks and becomes the leading edge of the wing. A small understated sun circle above the wing evokes Icarus. Keep the silhouette compact, bold and legible at small sizes, with deliberate negative space and balanced geometry. Below the symbol place the exact word "IQAR" (I Q A R), in a refined medium-weight geometric sans serif with restrained spacing.
Flat vector-like brand design rendered as a high-quality raster asset, warm muted gold #D5B563, genuinely transparent background so it works on the platform's near-black surfaces. Centered stacked symbol and wordmark, tightly composed with modest clear margins. No gradients, 3D, shadows, mockup, border, extra text, axes, dollar signs, candlesticks or literal human figure. The wing and financial curve should feel integrated rather than two clipart elements. Produce a single finished logo, not a presentation sheet.
